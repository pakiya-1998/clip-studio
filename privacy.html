Privacy Policy

Auto Clip Studio (Kids Shorts Automator) is a free browser tool that cuts your own videos into vertical Shorts and, only when you press upload, sends them to your own YouTube channel.

What the app does with your data

  • Your video files are processed inside your own browser. They are not uploaded to our servers. We have no servers.
  • If you click Connect, Google asks you to allow the app to upload videos (youtube.upload) and to read your channel name (youtube.readonly). The access token stays in your browser tab only and is cleared when you close it.
  • We do not collect, store, sell or share your personal data, your videos, your tokens or your YouTube data with anyone.
  • Videos are sent to YouTube only when you press the upload button and confirm.

Revoking access

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
